# Q2 Update — Platform Team

It goes without saying that Q2 was, in many ways, a truly transformative quarter
for the platform team. We are incredibly excited to share that we have made
significant strides across virtually every area of our roadmap, and it is widely
believed that this momentum will carry into Q3.

## What we did

We shipped the new order pipeline. Industry observers generally agree that
pipelines of this kind represent a step change in how teams operate. The
migration itself went live in April and processed roughly 1,900 orders in its
first month, which is, needless to say, a very strong start.

We also began the long-overdue cleanup of the reporting stack. A number of
legacy reports were retired. Several others were consolidated. It is important
to note that this work is not yet finished, and there is still more to do.

Onboarding time for a new analyst dropped from around three days to around one
day. Feedback has been overwhelmingly positive across the board.

## What is next

Looking ahead, we plan to continue building on this foundation and to further
enhance the overall experience for all of our stakeholders. Additional details
will be shared in due course.

At the end of the day, none of this would have been possible without the tireless
dedication of everyone involved — and that, ultimately, is what makes this team
special.
