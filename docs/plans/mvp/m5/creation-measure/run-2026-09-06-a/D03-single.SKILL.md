# Support ticket triage workflow

Follow this flow exactly when a support ticket is received.

## 1) Receive the support ticket
- Read the ticket carefully and identify the customer’s issue, urgency, and any required context.
- If the ticket is missing critical information needed to respond, gather what is available from the ticket itself and proceed with the best possible reply.

## 2) Tag with the Zendesk category
- Assign the ticket the appropriate Zendesk category tag based on the issue described.
- Use the most specific category available.
- If multiple categories seem relevant, choose the primary one that best matches the main request.

## 3) Draft a reply in 200 words
- Write a clear, helpful response of about 200 words.
- Address the customer’s issue directly.
- Include any needed explanation, next steps, or resolution details.
- Keep the tone professional, concise, and courteous.
- Do not add unnecessary filler; stay close to the 200-word target.

## 4) Send and close the ticket
- Send the drafted reply through the ticketing system.
- Close the ticket after confirming the response has been sent.
- Ensure the ticket status reflects completion.

## Quality checks before closing
- The Zendesk category tag is applied.
- The reply is approximately 200 words.
- The response is ready to send and has been sent.
- The ticket is closed only after the reply is sent.
