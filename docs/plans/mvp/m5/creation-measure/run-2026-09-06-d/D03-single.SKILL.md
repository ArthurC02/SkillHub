# Support ticket workflow

Follow the ticket in this order:

1. **Receive the support ticket**
   - Read the ticket carefully.
   - Identify the customer issue, urgency, and any missing information.

2. **Tag with the Zendesk category**
   - Assign the most appropriate Zendesk category tag based on the ticket content.
   - If the category is unclear, choose the closest valid category rather than inventing a new one.
   - Keep the tag consistent with the issue described in the ticket.

3. **Draft a reply in 200 words**
   - Write a clear, helpful response of about 200 words.
   - Address the customer’s issue directly.
   - Include any needed next steps, clarification requests, or resolution details.
   - Keep the tone professional, polite, and concise.
   - Do not exceed the requested length by much; aim for roughly 200 words.

4. **Send and close the ticket**
   - Send the drafted reply through the ticketing system.
   - Close the ticket after sending if no further action is required.
   - If the ticket cannot be resolved from the available information, state what is missing in the reply before closing or escalating according to the system’s normal process.

## Operating rules
- Do not skip steps.
- Do not close the ticket before drafting and sending the reply.
- Do not invent facts that are not supported by the ticket.
- If the ticket content is insufficient to determine the correct category or response, use the best available information and note the limitation in the reply.
