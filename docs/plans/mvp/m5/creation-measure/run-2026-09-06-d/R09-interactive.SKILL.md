# Resume-to-Data-Analyst One-Pager

## Purpose
Rewrite an existing resume into a one-page version targeted at data analyst openings. Use this skill when the user wants their resume reframed for a specific job family, especially when the source material must be preserved faithfully and no new facts may be added.

## What to preserve
- Keep only facts supported by the user's source resume or provided notes.
- Do not invent employers, titles, dates, metrics, tools, degrees, certifications, or accomplishments.
- If a fact is unclear, keep the uncertainty visible or flag it for clarification instead of guessing.

## How to rewrite
1. Read the source resume and identify the strongest evidence of data-analysis-relevant work.
2. Reorder content so the most relevant items appear first: summary, skills, experience, education, projects, and other relevant sections as available.
3. Rephrase bullets to emphasize analysis, reporting, experimentation, dashboards, data cleaning, business insight, SQL, spreadsheets, statistics, or stakeholder communication only when supported by the source.
4. Trim or de-emphasize unrelated content while keeping important career history intact.
5. Keep the final result concise enough to fit on one page when formatted normally.

## Writing rules
- Use a professional resume tone.
- Prefer concrete, outcome-oriented wording.
- Avoid hype and vague claims.
- Do not use filler just to reach a desired length.
- If the source material is sparse, write a compact resume and explicitly note what information is missing.

## Output structure
Produce a resume draft with the most appropriate sections for the source material. Common section order:
1. Name and contact details, if provided
2. Summary
3. Skills
4. Experience
5. Education
6. Projects or certifications, if supported by the source

## If information is insufficient
- Do not fabricate details.
- Keep the draft honest by leaving unsupported items out.
- Add a short note listing the missing details needed for a stronger data-analyst-targeted resume, such as tools used, measurable outcomes, or project examples.

## Quality check before finalizing
- The draft is clearly aimed at data analyst roles.
- The final length is one page or less in normal resume formatting.
- Every substantive claim is traceable to the source materials.
- The document reads like a resume, not a cover letter or personal statement.
