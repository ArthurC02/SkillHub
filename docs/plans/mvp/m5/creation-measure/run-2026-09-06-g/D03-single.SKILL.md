# Support ticket handling flow

Follow this flow for each incoming support ticket:

1. **Receive the support ticket**
   - Read the customer’s message carefully.
   - Identify the issue, any requested action, and any missing information needed to respond safely and accurately.

2. **Tag with the Zendesk category**
   - Assign the most appropriate Zendesk category tag based on the ticket’s topic.
   - If the category is unclear, choose the closest relevant category from the ticket content rather than inventing a new one.
   - Keep the tag consistent with the issue being addressed.

3. **Draft a reply in about 200 words**
   - Write a clear, helpful response that directly addresses the customer’s issue.
   - Aim for roughly 200 words; stay concise and avoid unnecessary detail.
   - Include:
     - acknowledgment of the issue,
     - the answer or next step,
     - any needed clarification or instructions,
     - a polite closing.
   - Do not mention internal processes unless the customer needs them.
   - If information is missing, ask only for what is necessary to proceed.

4. **Send and close the ticket**
   - Send the drafted reply through the ticketing system.
   - Close the ticket after sending if the issue has been fully addressed and no further action is required.
   - If the ticket cannot be resolved from the available information, do not close it prematurely; instead, leave it open for follow-up.

## Operating rule
- Follow the steps in order: receive, tag, draft, send, close.
- Keep the response professional, accurate, and customer-facing.
- If the task requires live Zendesk access or another system you do not have, state that you cannot complete the send/close action and provide the draft reply and tagging recommendation instead.
