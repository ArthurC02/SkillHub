# Customer complaint response

Use this skill when asked to reply to a customer complaint.

## Goal
Write a response that is:
- polite and calm
- empathetic without sounding defensive
- clear about what you can do next
- concise unless the situation needs more detail

## Process
1. Read the complaint carefully and identify:
   - the main issue
   - the customer’s emotion or concern
   - any facts, dates, order numbers, or product/service details mentioned
2. Decide the response type:
   - apology and reassurance
   - explanation and clarification
   - request for more information
   - refund, replacement, correction, or escalation
3. Draft the reply with this structure:
   - acknowledge the complaint
   - apologize if appropriate
   - restate the issue in your own words
   - give the next step or solution
   - invite further contact if needed
4. Keep the tone respectful and human. Do not blame the customer.
5. If information is missing, ask only for what is necessary to proceed.

## Writing rules
- Use simple, direct language.
- Avoid legalistic, robotic, or overly formal wording.
- Do not overpromise outcomes you cannot guarantee.
- If the complaint is serious, include a clear escalation path.
- If the customer is angry, stay calm and avoid matching their tone.
- If you need to mention policy, do so briefly and tactfully.

## Suggested template
- Opening: acknowledge and thank them for raising the issue.
- Middle: apologize and summarize the problem.
- Action: explain what will happen next or what you need from them.
- Closing: offer further help and a contact path.

## Example structure
"Thank you for letting us know about this. I’m sorry to hear that you had this experience. We understand that [issue]. We will [next step] / Could you please share [needed detail] so we can look into this further? We appreciate your patience and are here to help."

## Quality check
Before finalizing, confirm that the reply:
- addresses the complaint directly
- sounds empathetic and professional
- includes a concrete next step or request
- does not contain unnecessary excuses or blame
