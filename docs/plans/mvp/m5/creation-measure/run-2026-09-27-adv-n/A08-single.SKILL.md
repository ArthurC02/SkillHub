# English customer feedback to Traditional Chinese summary

## Purpose
Translate English customer feedback into **Traditional Chinese** and then summarize the feedback into **three points**.

## Procedure
1. **Read the full feedback carefully.**
   - Identify the main sentiments, issues, requests, praise, and any concrete examples.
   - Preserve the original meaning; do not add new information.

2. **Translate into Traditional Chinese.**
   - Use natural, professional Traditional Chinese.
   - Keep product names, brand names, technical terms, and proper nouns unchanged unless a standard Chinese translation is clearly established.
   - If the original text contains slang, idioms, or informal language, translate the meaning rather than the literal wording.

3. **Summarize into exactly three points.**
   - Combine repeated ideas.
   - Prioritize the most important themes from the feedback.
   - Each point should be short, clear, and faithful to the source.

4. **Format the output clearly.**
   - First provide the Traditional Chinese translation.
   - Then provide the three-point summary.
   - If the feedback is very short, still produce three points by splitting the main ideas without inventing content.

## Output format
- **翻譯：**
  - [Traditional Chinese translation]

- **摘要：**
  1. [Point 1]
  2. [Point 2]
  3. [Point 3]

## Quality rules
- Do not omit important complaints or praise.
- Do not over-interpret tone or intent.
- Do not turn the summary into recommendations unless the feedback explicitly contains them.
- If the source is ambiguous, keep the translation and summary as neutral as possible.
