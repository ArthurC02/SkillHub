# Support ticket triage workflow

Follow the workflow in order for each incoming support ticket.

1. **Receive the support ticket**
   - Read the ticket carefully and identify the customer issue, context, and any required action.
   - If the ticket is missing essential information, note the gap in your draft reply so it can be requested clearly.

2. **Tag with the Zendesk category**
   - Assign the most appropriate Zendesk category tag based on the ticket’s subject and content.
   - Use the category that best matches the primary issue; if multiple categories seem possible, choose the most specific one.
   - If your environment requires a standardized tag format, apply that format consistently.

3. **Draft a reply in 200 words**
   - Write a helpful, professional response of about 200 words.
   - Address the customer’s issue directly, acknowledge their concern, and state the next step or resolution.
   - Keep the tone clear, polite, and concise.
   - Do not exceed the requested length by much; aim for roughly 200 words.
   - If information is missing, ask for only the minimum needed details.

4. **Send and close the ticket**
   - Review the tag and reply for accuracy, tone, and completeness.
   - Send the reply through the ticketing system.
   - Close the ticket after sending, unless the ticket clearly requires escalation or additional internal action that cannot be completed from the available information.

## Quality checks
- The category tag matches the ticket’s main issue.
- The reply is approximately 200 words.
- The reply is professional and actionable.
- The ticket is sent and closed only after the response is ready.
