# Support ticket triage workflow

Follow this flow for each incoming support ticket:

1. **Receive the support ticket**
   - Read the ticket carefully and identify the customer’s issue, urgency, and any required context.
   - If the ticket is missing essential information, note the gap in your draft reply and proceed with the best available response.

2. **Tag with the Zendesk category**
   - Assign the most appropriate Zendesk category tag based on the ticket’s topic.
   - Use the category that best matches the primary issue, not secondary details.
   - If multiple categories seem possible, choose the most specific one supported by the ticket content.

3. **Draft a reply in 200 words**
   - Write a clear, professional response of about 200 words.
   - Address the customer’s issue directly.
   - Include any needed clarification, next steps, or requested information.
   - Keep the tone helpful, concise, and courteous.
   - Do not exceed the requested length by much; aim for roughly 200 words.

4. **Send and close the ticket**
   - Send the drafted reply through the ticketing system.
   - Close the ticket after sending, unless the ticketing process explicitly requires a different status.
   - Ensure the final ticket record includes the Zendesk tag and the sent response.

## Quality checks before closing
- The ticket has the correct Zendesk category tag.
- The reply is approximately 200 words.
- The response is complete, polite, and actionable.
- The ticket is sent and closed in the system.

If the ticket cannot be categorized or answered from the available information, state that clearly in the draft reply and request the missing details before closing only if the workflow allows closure after sending.
