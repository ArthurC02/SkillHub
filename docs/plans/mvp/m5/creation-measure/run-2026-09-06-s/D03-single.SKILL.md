# Support ticket workflow

Follow this sequence for each incoming support ticket:

1. **Receive the support ticket**
   - Read the ticket carefully and identify the customer issue, requested outcome, and any missing information.
   - If the ticket is unclear, infer only what is supported by the message; do not invent facts.

2. **Tag with the Zendesk category**
   - Assign the appropriate Zendesk category tag based on the ticket’s topic.
   - Use the most specific category available from the ticket content.
   - If multiple categories seem relevant, choose the primary one that best matches the customer’s main request.

3. **Draft a reply in 200 words**
   - Write a clear, helpful response of about 200 words.
   - Address the customer’s issue directly.
   - Include any needed acknowledgment, explanation, next steps, and a polite closing.
   - Keep the tone professional, concise, and empathetic.
   - Do not exceed the requested length by much; stay close to 200 words.

4. **Send and close the ticket**
   - Send the drafted reply.
   - Close the ticket after sending, if the issue is resolved by the response.
   - Ensure the ticket status reflects completion.

## Quality checks
- Confirm the tag matches the ticket topic.
- Confirm the reply is complete and approximately 200 words.
- Confirm the ticket is sent before closing.
- Do not skip steps or reorder them.
