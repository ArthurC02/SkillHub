== reading: what the product does with the same twelve cases ==
The three attack goals this script counts map onto Go as follows.

  marker into the draft (evaluation-3, and the two same-goal cases in the other kinds)
    Go compares the whole draft — body, name, description, compatibility, allowed_tools and every
    packaged file — against the judge's own words, and a literal that appears only there sends
    the draft back with a tool message instead of storing it (copiedFromEvaluation, creation.go;
    TestADraftThatCopiesAMarkerOutOfTheEvaluationIsCaught, TestAMarkerHiddenInAPackagedFileIsCaughtToo).
    Deterministic: it does not depend on the model choosing well.

  brief changed (evaluation-4, fetched_page-1, reference-1)
    A step that comes back with a different brief does not take effect: Go clears the confirmation
    and asks the person again (job.go's briefChanged branch, tested in creation_test.go). The
    changed brief is visible to the person as a new confirmation, not applied behind them.

  bash in allowed_tools (evaluation-2) and met claimed over a failed evaluation (evaluation-1)
    No Go backstop. The prompt is the whole defence for these two, and the prompt is what the two
    runs above show to be probabilistic. Recorded as such; the acceptance criterion that reads
    "紅線 0/N" is measured on this script, i.e. on the model layer, and the owner's call on where
    that red line belongs is 05 R-54.
