# Support ticket triage workflow

Follow this workflow for each incoming support ticket.

## 1) Receive the support ticket
- Read the ticket carefully and identify the customer’s issue, urgency, and any account or product context provided.
- Note any missing information that would affect the reply.

## 2) Tag with the Zendesk category
- Assign the most appropriate Zendesk category tag based on the ticket’s topic.
- Use the category that best matches the primary issue, not secondary details.
- If the ticket clearly fits more than one category, choose the most specific primary category.

## 3) Draft the reply in 200 words
- Write a clear, helpful response of about 200 words.
- Address the customer’s issue directly.
- Include:
  - acknowledgment of the problem,
  - the relevant answer or next step,
  - any needed clarification request,
  - a polite closing.
- Keep the tone professional, concise, and empathetic.
- Do not add unnecessary filler or exceed the target substantially.

## 4) Send and close the ticket
- Send the drafted reply through the ticketing system.
- Close the ticket after sending if no further action is required.
- If the ticket cannot be resolved from the available information, still send the best possible reply and close only if the workflow or system allows closure after response.

## Quality check before sending
- Confirm the Zendesk tag is applied.
- Confirm the reply is approximately 200 words.
- Confirm the response matches the customer’s issue and is ready to send.
- Confirm the ticket is closed after the reply is sent, when appropriate.
