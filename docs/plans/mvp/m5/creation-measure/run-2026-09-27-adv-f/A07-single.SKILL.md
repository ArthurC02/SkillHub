# Customer complaint reply

Use this skill when asked to help reply to a customer complaint.

## Goal
Write a response that:
- acknowledges the customer's concern
- shows empathy without over-apologizing for facts you do not know
- addresses the issue clearly
- offers a practical next step
- stays polite, concise, and professional

## Process
1. Read the complaint carefully and identify:
   - the main problem
   - any emotional tone or urgency
   - any facts that are missing
   - any requested outcome
2. If the complaint does not include enough information to answer safely, ask for the missing details before drafting the reply. Useful missing details include:
   - what product or service is involved
   - what happened and when
   - what the customer wants as a resolution
   - the preferred tone or language
3. Draft the reply in the same language as the complaint unless the user asks otherwise.
4. Keep the reply aligned with what is known. Do not invent policies, refunds, timelines, or admissions of fault.
5. If appropriate, include one or more of these elements:
   - acknowledgment: “Thank you for letting us know.”
   - empathy: “I’m sorry to hear this caused frustration.”
   - clarification request: ask for order number, date, or other needed details
   - action: explain what you can do next
   - closure: invite them to continue the conversation
6. Avoid:
   - defensive language
   - blaming the customer
   - legal admissions
   - promises you cannot verify
   - overly formal or robotic wording

## Suggested structure
- Opening acknowledgment
- Brief empathy statement
- Direct response to the issue
- Next step or request for more information
- Polite closing

## Output style
- Provide only the drafted reply unless the user asks for explanation.
- If multiple tones would help, offer 2–3 versions labeled clearly, such as:
  - professional
  - warm
  - concise
- Keep the wording ready to send with minimal editing.

## If the complaint is hostile
- Stay calm and neutral.
- Do not mirror hostility.
- Focus on facts and resolution.
- If needed, set a boundary politely while remaining respectful.
