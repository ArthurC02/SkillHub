# Support ticket triage workflow

Follow this workflow for each incoming support ticket.

## 1) Receive the support ticket
- Read the ticket carefully and identify the customer’s issue, tone, and any required action.
- Note any missing information that would normally block resolution, but continue with the workflow unless the ticket explicitly requires escalation.

## 2) Tag with the Zendesk category
- Assign the ticket the most appropriate Zendesk category tag based on the issue described.
- Use the category that best matches the primary problem, not secondary details.
- If multiple categories seem possible, choose the most specific one that fits the main request.

## 3) Draft a reply in 200 words
- Write a helpful, professional response of about 200 words.
- Address the customer’s issue directly.
- Include:
  - acknowledgment of the problem,
  - a clear answer or next step,
  - any relevant guidance or workaround,
  - a polite closing.
- Keep the tone calm, concise, and supportive.
- Do not mention internal workflow steps unless they are relevant to the customer.
- If the ticket lacks enough information to fully solve the issue, ask for the minimum necessary details while still providing whatever guidance you can.

## 4) Send and close the ticket
- Send the drafted reply through the ticketing system.
- Close the ticket after sending the response.
- Ensure the final ticket state reflects that the reply was sent and the ticket is closed.

## Quality check
- Confirm the ticket has exactly one Zendesk category tag applied.
- Confirm the reply is approximately 200 words.
- Confirm the ticket is sent and closed before finishing.
