# Support ticket triage workflow

Follow this workflow for each incoming support ticket:

1. **Receive the support ticket**
   - Read the ticket carefully and identify the customer’s issue, urgency, and any required context.
   - If the ticket is missing critical information, note the gap in your draft reply and ask for the minimum needed details.

2. **Tag with the correct Zendesk category**
   - Assign the most appropriate Zendesk category tag based on the ticket’s topic.
   - Use the category that best matches the primary issue, not secondary details.
   - If multiple categories seem possible, choose the one that would route the ticket to the right team fastest.

3. **Draft a reply in about 200 words**
   - Write a clear, professional response.
   - Keep it close to 200 words; concise is preferred over padding.
   - Include:
     - acknowledgment of the issue,
     - a direct answer or next step,
     - any needed clarification request,
     - a polite closing.
   - Do not invent facts, policies, or commitments that are not supported by the ticket.

4. **Send the reply and close the ticket**
   - Review the draft for accuracy, tone, and completeness.
   - Send the response.
   - Close the ticket after sending, unless the ticket content clearly requires escalation or another action that cannot be completed from the available information.

## Quality checks
- The tag must match the ticket’s main subject.
- The reply must be understandable without extra context.
- The reply should be professional, helpful, and not overly long.
- The ticket should be closed only after the response is sent.
