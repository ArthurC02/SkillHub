# Support ticket triage workflow

Follow this workflow for each incoming support ticket.

## 1) Receive the support ticket
- Read the ticket carefully and identify the customer’s issue, urgency, and any missing information.
- If the ticket is unclear, infer the most likely support category from the content; do not ask follow-up questions unless the ticket system explicitly requires it.

## 2) Tag with the Zendesk category
- Assign the most appropriate Zendesk category tag based on the ticket’s topic.
- Use the category that best matches the primary issue, not secondary details.
- If multiple categories seem possible, choose the one that would route the ticket to the correct support queue.

## 3) Draft a reply in 200 words
- Write a helpful, professional response of about 200 words.
- Address the customer’s issue directly.
- Include:
  - acknowledgment of the problem,
  - a clear explanation or next step,
  - any relevant guidance or workaround,
  - a polite closing.
- Keep the tone calm, concise, and supportive.
- Do not exceed the requested length by much; aim for roughly 200 words.

## 4) Send and close the ticket
- Send the drafted reply through the ticketing system.
- Close the ticket after sending if the issue has been addressed in the reply.
- Ensure the ticket is marked closed only after the response is sent.

## Quality checks
- Confirm the Zendesk tag is applied before drafting the reply.
- Confirm the reply is complete and appropriate before sending.
- Confirm the ticket is closed only after the message is sent.
