# Support ticket triage workflow

Follow this flow for each incoming support ticket:

1. **Receive the support ticket**
   - Read the ticket carefully and identify the customer issue, requested outcome, and any relevant context.
   - Note any missing information that would affect the reply.

2. **Tag with the Zendesk category**
   - Assign the most appropriate Zendesk category tag based on the ticket content.
   - Use the category that best matches the primary issue, not secondary details.
   - If the ticket clearly fits more than one category, choose the most specific one available.

3. **Draft the reply in 200 words**
   - Write a clear, helpful response of about 200 words.
   - Address the customer’s issue directly.
   - Include any needed explanation, next steps, or resolution.
   - Keep the tone professional, concise, and courteous.
   - Do not add unnecessary filler; stay close to the requested length.

4. **Send and close the ticket**
   - Send the drafted reply through the ticketing system.
   - Close the ticket after sending if no further action is required.
   - If the ticket cannot be resolved from the available information, do not invent facts; instead, note what is needed before closure.

## Quality checks before closing
- The ticket has the correct Zendesk category tag.
- The reply is approximately 200 words.
- The response is complete, polite, and actionable.
- The ticket is sent and closed only after the reply is ready.
